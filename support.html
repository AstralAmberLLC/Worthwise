Common Questions

  • Is Worthwise Pocket available now? Worthwise is Availiable to download for free in the App Store.
  • Does Worthwise Pocket collect the data I input in the App? Worthwise Pocket does not collect any data from the app, your data is secured using iOS sandbox system from Apple.

  • If I delete the App can I recover the data I had in there? Only if the user previously made an encrypted backup and saved it within its device's storage, the data can be restored, Otherwise there's no way data can be recovered.
